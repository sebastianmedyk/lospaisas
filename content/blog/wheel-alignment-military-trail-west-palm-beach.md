## What are the signs you need an alignment?

The most common signs are:

- The car drifts left or right on a straight, level road
- The steering wheel is crooked when you drive straight
- Inner or outer tread wears faster than the rest
- The ride feels “off” after hitting a curb, pothole, or rough stretch near Southern Boulevard or I-95

Alignment alone does not fix every vibration — that is often **balancing** — but it helps tires track correctly and last longer when the geometry is out of spec.

## Why does alignment matter for tire life?

Wheels that are out of alignment wear the tread unevenly, so skipping an alignment after new tires, or after a hard hit, can wear the tires out early. ## Questions about wheel alignment

### Is a vibration an alignment problem?

Usually not. A shake at highway speed that fades when you slow down is more often balancing. A steady pull to one side is more often alignment.

### Should I get an alignment with new tires?

If the old tires wore unevenly or the car pulls, ask about alignment when the new tires go on, so the new set does not wear the same way.
