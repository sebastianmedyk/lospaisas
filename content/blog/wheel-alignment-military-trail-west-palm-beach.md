## Signs you may need alignment

Drivers along Military Trail and across Palm Beach County often notice:

- The car drifts left or right on a straight, level road
- The steering wheel is crooked when you drive straight
- Inner or outer tread wears faster than the rest
- The ride feels “off” after hitting a curb, pothole, or rough stretch near Southern Boulevard or I-95

Alignment alone does not fix every vibration — that is often **balancing** — but it helps tires track correctly and last longer when the geometry is out of spec.
