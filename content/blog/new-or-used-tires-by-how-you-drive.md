New or used tires: which one makes sense for you? The answer depends less on the tire and more on **how you drive**. At **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach we explain both options in your size, without pressure.

## When new tires fit better

- You drive a lot of highway miles every day
- You plan to keep the car for years
- You want the full tread life and a matching set

## When used tires fit better

- You need a safe tire at a lower cost (used from $65)
- You are replacing one damaged tire and want to match the others' wear
- You plan to sell the car soon or drive mostly short local trips

## Either way, checked and balanced

Every used tire is checked for tread, sidewall, and DOT date before we sell it. New or used, we mount and balance on site.
