A flat can be repaired when the damage is a small puncture in the tread and the rest of the tire is sound; a cut or hole in the sidewall means the tire is replaced. At **Los Paisas Tires Shop** on **S Military Trl in West Palm Beach**, we offer **tire repair and vulcanization** when the damage is in a safe, repairable zone — and we tell you clearly when it is not.

## How does the shop decide between repair and replacement?

We decide by where the damage is and how bad it is. We look at:

- Location of the hole (tread vs. sidewall)
- Size and type of damage
- Overall tread depth and casing condition
- Whether the tire has prior repairs that limit options

If vulcanization or a proper repair will hold safely, we explain the process and the price up front. If the sidewall is cut, the tread is too thin, or the casing is compromised, we recommend **new** or carefully inspected **used** tires instead of a risky patch.

## Questions about tire repair

### Can a sidewall puncture be repaired?

No. A cut, bubble, or hole in the sidewall is not repairable. The tire is replaced with a new or inspected used tire in your size.

### What does vulcanization mean at this shop?

The tire comes off the wheel and the injury is sealed from the inside. If that cannot be done safely, the tire is replaced instead.
