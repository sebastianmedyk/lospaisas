Does your work truck or SUV need tires? Light trucks that carry tools, materials, or a trailer need **LT (light truck) tires**, made for heavier loads and daily work. **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach keeps LT sizes along with passenger tires.

## LT vs. P-metric tires

- **LT** at the start of the size (e.g., LT265/70 R17) means a light-truck tire with a stronger build and a **load range** (C, D, E)
- **P** or no letter means a passenger tire
- If your truck came with LT tires, replace them with LT tires of the same or higher load range

### Can I put passenger tires on my work truck?

If the truck came with LT tires or carries heavy loads, stay with LT tires of the right load range.

### What does load range E mean?

It is a stronger LT build rated for heavier loads than C or D, at the right pressure.
