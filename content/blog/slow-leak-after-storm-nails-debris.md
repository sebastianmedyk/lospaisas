After a storm, nails, screws, and debris end up all over the road, and a **slow leak can show up a day or two later**. If your tire keeps losing air, bring it to **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach.

## Signs of a slow leak

- The tire pressure light comes back after you fill up
- One tire looks lower than the others in the morning
- You find a nail or screw head in the tread

## Repair or replace?

We find the leak first. If the puncture is **in the tread**, we repair it. If the hole is **too close to the sidewall** or the tire was driven flat, we replace it with a new or used tire in your size (used from $65) and balance it the same visit. A slow leak can also come from a bent rim or the valve stem, so we check those too.

## Don't leave the nail in for weeks

Driving on a tire that is always low heats it up and can ruin a tire that was repairable.

## Questions

### Should I pull the nail out myself?

No. Leave it in and drive to the shop; pulling it can turn a slow leak into a flat.

### How do you find a slow leak?

We inflate the tire and check the tread, valve, and rim edge for escaping air.
