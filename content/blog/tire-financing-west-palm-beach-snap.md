## Why waiting on a bad tire costs more

When money is tight, it is tempting to drive on a tire that is bald, has a slow leak or a damaged sidewall "for a few more weeks." That is usually the expensive choice. A worn tire grips poorly in the rain, a slow leak can ruin the tire completely, and a sidewall bulge can fail at highway speed. A blowout can also damage the wheel.

Getting a safe tire now, and spreading the cost, is often better than waiting for a breakdown that costs more and happens at the worst moment.
