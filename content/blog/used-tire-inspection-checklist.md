## What we check on each used tire

- **Tread depth:** enough tread left to be worth your money
- **Sidewall:** no cuts, cracks, bulges, or old plugs near the shoulder
- **DOT date:** the last four digits show how old the tire is
- **Inside and bead:** no signs of the tire being driven flat

## Why it matters

A used tire is only a good deal if it is safe. The check takes minutes and saves you from buying a tire that fails a few weeks later.
