Wheels need balancing because a tire and wheel are almost never perfectly even, and that uneven weight shows up as vibration in the steering wheel or seat. At **Los Paisas Tires Shop** on S Military Trl in West Palm Beach, the price of the balance is stated before the weights go on, so there are **no hidden fees**.

## What happens if wheels are not balanced?

Unbalanced wheels can cause:

- Steering shake at highway speeds
- Uneven tread wear
- Extra stress on suspension parts

A proper balance helps your tires last longer and keeps the ride smoother on I-95, Southern Boulevard, and local Palm Beach County roads.

## What does “no hidden fees” mean at this shop?

We tell you the price for balancing up front. No mystery add-ons after the wheels are already on the machine. If a wheel is bent and will not balance, or a tire is separated inside, we stop and tell you instead of charging for a balance that cannot work.

## Questions about wheel balancing

### How do I know if I need balancing or an alignment?

A shake at highway speed that fades when you slow down usually points to balancing. A constant pull to the right or left is more often alignment. Tell us which one you feel.

### Will I know the balancing price first?

Yes. The price of the balance is stated before the weights go on.

### Can you balance tires I bought somewhere else?

Yes. The same no-hidden-fees rule applies whether you bought the tire here or brought it in.

### Can the mobile service balance my wheels?
