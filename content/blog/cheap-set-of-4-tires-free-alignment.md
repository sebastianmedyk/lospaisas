## Two tires or four? How we decide with you

Not every car needs four new tires at once. Sometimes two are enough. The decision depends on a few simple things we look at with you:

- **How much tread is left on the other tires.** If two tires are worn and two are still deep, replacing the worn pair may be enough.
- **Whether the wear is even.** Uneven wear across all four often points to alignment, and putting new tires on without fixing that wastes money.
- **Your car.** Some vehicles, for example many all-wheel-drive cars, are sensitive to tires with very different tread depths.
- **How you drive.** Long daily commutes and highway trips put more demand on the tires than short trips around town.

We tell you honestly whether you need two or four, and we do not push a full set when a pair will do. For a deeper look, read [do I need four new tires?](/en/blog/do-i-need-four-new-tires).
