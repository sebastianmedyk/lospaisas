Driving on the spare for a week? That small **donut spare** is made to get you to a tire shop, not for daily driving. **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach can get a full-size tire back on, usually in one visit.

## The limits of a donut spare

The limits are printed on the spare's sidewall or a label; many are rated for **50 mph**, and makers usually advise short distances only. A temporary spare has less tread and a different size, so braking and handling change, and it can strain the drivetrain on some vehicles.

## What we do

- If the damage on your flat is **in the tread**, we repair it
- If not, we put on a **new or used tire in your size** (used from $65)
- We balance it, so the spare goes back in the trunk

## Check the spare afterward

Spares lose air sitting in the trunk. Ask us to check its pressure before you leave.
