Driving to family for Thanksgiving or another holiday trip? Check your tires before the long drive. A problem that is minor around town gets worse after hours on I-95 or the Turnpike. **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach can check them the same day.

## A 5-minute check before you leave

- **Tread depth:** worn tires grip poorly in rain
- **Sidewalls:** cuts, cracks, or bulges mean replace before the trip
- **Pressure:** set to the door sticker, including the spare
- **Slow leak:** one tire always lower than the others
- **Vibration:** a shake at highway speed usually means balancing
