## When does a used tire make sense?

A used tire makes sense when you need to get back on the road quickly and the casing passes inspection: getting off a spare, keeping a daily driver going, or stretching the budget between paychecks. New tires are ideal when the budget allows. The key is inspection, not just the sticker price.
