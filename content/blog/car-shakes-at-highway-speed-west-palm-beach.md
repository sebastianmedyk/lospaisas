A car that shakes at highway speed, in the steering wheel or in the seat, most often has a wheel that is out of balance, not an alignment problem. ## What does a highway-speed shake usually mean?

A tire and wheel are almost never perfectly even. When the weight is not spread evenly around the wheel, the assembly hops a little every time it turns. At low speed you barely notice. As speed builds, the hop turns into a vibration you feel through the car. The classic sign is a shake that shows up on I-95 or the Turnpike and fades again when you slow down.

Unbalanced tires do more than make the ride uncomfortable. The uneven contact wears the tread faster than it should, so a vibration you ignore today can cost you tread later.

## Is the shake in the steering wheel or in the seat?

Where you feel it is a useful clue, and it is worth telling us when you arrive.

- **Steering wheel shake** usually points to the front wheels.
- **Seat or floor vibration** more often comes from the rear wheels.

This is a general rule, not a diagnosis. Los Paisas Tires Shop still puts the wheels on the balancer to see which one is actually off, because guessing leads to paying for work that does not fix anything.

## Is it balancing or alignment?

Many drivers come in asking for an alignment when the real problem is balance. The two feel different:

| What you feel | More likely cause | What the shop checks |
|---|---|---|
| Shake at highway speed that fades when you slow down | Wheel balance | Each wheel on the balancer |
| Steering wheel shake | Front wheel balance | Front wheels first |
| Seat or floor vibration | Rear wheel balance | Rear wheels |
| Car pulls to one side on a straight road | Alignment | Alignment (geometría) |
| Tread wearing on one shoulder | Alignment | Tread pattern and alignment |
| Shake that started after a curb or pothole hit | Bent wheel or damaged tire | Wheel and tire condition |

If the car pulls and also shakes, both may be needed. We explain which one first, in plain words, before any work starts. See [signs your car needs a wheel alignment](/blog/wheel-alignment-military-trail-west-palm-beach) for the pulling side of the story.

## What else can cause a vibration at speed?

Balance is the common answer, but not the only one. Other ordinary causes the shop looks for:

- A balance weight that fell off
- A wheel bent by a curb or pothole
- A tire damaged inside, which weights cannot make smooth
- A tire that was mounted, repaired, or moved from another car and never balanced afterward

If a wheel is bent or a tire is damaged inside, Los Paisas Tires Shop tells you instead of charging for a balance that cannot work. If the shake only appears when you brake, that points away from the tires. This shop works on tires only and will say so.

## How often should wheels be balanced?

Los Paisas Tires Shop recommends balancing every time you put on new or used tires, and again every few thousand miles so a vibration does not sneak up on you. A balance also makes sense after a tire repair or after you hit something hard enough to feel it.

## Questions about a car that shakes at speed

### Why does my car only shake above a certain speed?

An unbalanced wheel hops a little each turn. At low speed that is too small to feel. At highway speed it becomes a steady vibration, which is why the shake often appears on the highway and fades when you slow down.

### Can I keep driving with a vibration?

A mild vibration is not an emergency, but it wears tires faster and can hide a damaged tire or bent wheel. Have it checked soon rather than waiting for it to get worse.
