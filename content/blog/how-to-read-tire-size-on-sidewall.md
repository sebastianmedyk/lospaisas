Need tires but not sure what size to ask for? The answer is printed on the side of the tire you already have. **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach explains the code in one minute.

## What 205/55 R16 means

- **205:** tire width in millimeters
- **55:** sidewall height as a percentage of the width
- **R:** radial construction
- **16:** rim diameter in inches

After the size you may see a load index and speed rating, like **91V**. Keep those the same or higher when replacing.

## Where else to find it

The sticker on the driver's door jamb lists the original size for your vehicle. If the tires on the car were changed, the sidewall shows what is on it now.
