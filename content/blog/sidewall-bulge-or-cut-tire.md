See a bubble or a cut on the side of your tire? That damage **cannot be patched**. At **Los Paisas Tires Shop** in West Palm Beach we check it and, if needed, replace it the same visit.

## Why sidewall damage is different

A plug or patch is only approved for punctures in the **tread area**. The sidewall flexes with every turn of the wheel, and a bulge means the inner cords are already broken. That tire can fail suddenly, especially at highway speed and in Florida heat.

## What to do

- Avoid the highway and drive slowly to the shop, or put on the spare
- Do not over-inflate the tire to "fix" the bulge
- Check the other tires: the same curb or pothole may have hit more than one

## Same-visit replacement

We replace the damaged tire with a **new or used tire in your size** (used from $65) and balance it so the ride stays smooth.

## Questions

### Can I drive on a tire with a small bulge?

It is not safe. Drive only as far as needed at low speed, or use the spare.

### Do I need to replace both tires on the axle?

Not always. We compare tread depth and tell you whether one tire is enough.

### What causes a sidewall bulge?

Usually an impact with a curb or pothole, or driving on a low tire.
