## What can the shop do for a flat tire on Sunday?

On Sunday the shop does the same tire work as any other day. Depending on the damage, we can:

- **Repair** and **vulcanize** when the puncture is repairable

### Can a flat tire be repaired on a Sunday?

Yes, when the puncture is in a repairable part of the tread. Repair and vulcanization are done any day the shop is open. A cut or hole in the sidewall is not repairable, so that tire is replaced with a new or used one.

### Do you do oil changes or brakes on Sunday?

No. Los Paisas Tires Shop works on tires only, on any day. Oil changes, brakes, and A/C are not offered.
