## Why do some shops push a full set?

The most expensive option is the easiest one to sell. A full set of new tires is one quick answer that ends the conversation. It is not always wrong, but it is often more than the car needs.

A lot of people come to Los Paisas Tires Shop after hearing that line, and the real problem turns out to be a single nail in the tread. We do not work that way. We look at each tire, explain what we see, and say it straight, with no unnecessary upselling.

## How does the shop decide what you actually need?

The decision depends on the condition of each tire, not on a rule that every job means four tires. The shop checks:

- **Where the damage is.** A puncture in the tread is usually repairable. Damage on the sidewall usually means replacement, for safety.
- **How deep the damage goes**, and whether the tire was driven flat.
- **Tread left on the other tires**, so a new or used tire matches the rest closely enough to keep the car stable.
- **Age and condition**, including cracks, bulges, or uneven wear.

Then you hear the options, the reason for each, and the price, before any work starts.

## Repair, one tire, two tires, or a full set?

| Situation | Often enough | When more is needed |
|---|---|---|
| Nail or screw in the tread | Tire repair or vulcanization | Damage too close to the sidewall, or tire driven flat |
| Sidewall cut or bulge | Replace that one tire | Other tires also worn or damaged |
| One tire ruined, others in good shape | One new or one good used tire matched to the rest | Remaining tread too uneven to match |
| Two tires worn, two still good | Two tires, with the fresher tread on the rear axle | Car or tread condition calls for a matched set |
| All four worn down or aging | A full set, new or used | — |

## Can I replace only two tires?

Yes, often. The rule Los Paisas Tires Shop gives every customer on a tight budget: if you replace only two, put the fresher tread on the **rear axle**, not the front. In Florida rain, a slicker rear tire is what makes a car fishtail and lose control, even when the front tires look worse. The shop balances whatever it installs so the ride feels right.

## Is a used tire a safe option?

A good used tire can be the right answer when one tire is ruined and the rest are fine. At Los Paisas Tires Shop every used tire is checked for tread and safety before it goes on a car, and the shop carries economic used tires as well as new ones. ## Questions about replacing tires

### Do I have to replace all four tires at once?

No. If only one or two tires are damaged or worn and the others are in good shape, replacing those can be enough. The shop checks that the tread matches closely enough to keep the car stable.

### Can a tire with a nail be repaired instead of replaced?

Often, yes. A puncture in the tread is usually repairable. Damage on the sidewall usually is not, so the shop checks the location and depth before deciding.

### Where should two new tires go?

On the rear axle. Fresher tread in the back helps keep the car from sliding in rain.

### Will you tell me if I don't need new tires?

Yes. If a repair or one tire is enough, that is what Los Paisas Tires Shop recommends.
