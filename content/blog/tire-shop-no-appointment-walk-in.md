No. **Los Paisas Tires Shop**, at 1114 S Military Trl in West Palm Beach, works on a walk-in basis for new and used tires, tire repair, balancing, and alignment. Come any time between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM, seven days a week. Mornings tend to be quieter if you want in and out fast.

## Which services can I walk in for?

You can show up without booking for the shop's everyday tire work:

- **New tires** and **used tires**, with every used tire checked for tread and safety before it goes on
- **Tire repair** and vulcanization for punctures
- **Wheel balancing**
- **Wheel alignment** (geometría)

Customers are served in order of arrival. Most jobs are done the same day, so in many cases you drive away with the work finished.

## When is the best time to come in?

Any time the shop is open works: **8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Sunday**, including weekends. If you want the shortest wait, mornings are usually quieter. If your schedule is tight, call **(561) 429-4041** before you leave to check whether the shop is busy at that moment.

## What should I bring or know before I walk in?

Nothing special is required. It helps to:

- Know where you feel the problem: a shake in the steering wheel or seat, a pull to one side, or a tire losing air
- If there is a nail, leave it in, and only drive in if the tire is not already going flat
- Bring tires you bought somewhere else if you want them mounted

While you wait there is Wi‑Fi, and you can pay by credit, debit, or NFC tap. The team explains the work and the price before anything starts, in English or Spanish.

## What if I can't drive to the shop?

Walk-in only works if the car can get there safely. If a tire is flat on the rim or the car should not be moved, call or WhatsApp **(561) 429-4041** about mobile tire service within 10 miles of the shop. See [how to request mobile tire service](/en/blog/mobile-tire-service-west-palm-beach).
