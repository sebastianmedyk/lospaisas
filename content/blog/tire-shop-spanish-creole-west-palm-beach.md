Yes. **Los Paisas Tires Shop**, at 1114 S Military Trl in West Palm Beach, serves customers in **Spanish, English, and Creole**. The team explains tire choices, balancing, alignment, and repairs in the language you prefer, before any work starts, seven days a week from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

## Why does language matter at a tire shop?

Tire work comes with words most drivers never use: tread depth, sidewall, load rating, balancing, alignment, vulcanization. If you cannot follow the explanation, you cannot judge whether a repair is enough or whether you really need a new tire. That is how people end up paying for work they did not need, or skipping work they did.

Los Paisas Tires Shop is a local shop on S Military Trail that serves the whole community around it. Explaining things clearly, with patience and without unnecessary jargon, is part of the job, not a favor.

## Which languages does Los Paisas Tires Shop speak?

The shop serves customers in three languages:

- **Spanish** (español)
- **English**
- **Creole** (criollo)

You do not need to ask ahead or book anything special. Tell the person at the counter which language you are comfortable in, and the conversation continues in that language.

## What does the shop explain before the work?

Before anything is touched, you hear what the car needs and why. In practice that covers:

| Situation | What gets explained |
|---|---|
| Choosing tires | New or used, what fits your size and budget, and why |
| Flat or nail | Whether the tire can be repaired or should be replaced, and where the damage is |
| Vibration at speed | Whether it is balancing, alignment, or a damaged tire or wheel |
| Car pulls to one side | What an alignment (geometría) corrects |
| Tires you bought elsewhere | What installing and balancing them involves |
| Price | What the job costs, stated before the work begins |

The goal is simple: you leave knowing exactly what was done to your car and why, without rush.

## What services can I get explained in my language?

Los Paisas Tires Shop works on tires only:

- New and used tires, with every used tire checked for tread and safety
- Tire repair and vulcanization
- Wheel balancing
- Wheel alignment (geometría)
- Installing tires you bought somewhere else
- Mobile tire service within 10 miles of the shop

The shop does not do oil changes, brakes, or A/C. If your problem is outside tires, the team will tell you that plainly instead of guessing.
